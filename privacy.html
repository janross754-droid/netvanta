Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NetVanta handles information when you visit our website, create an account, add money to your wallet, contact support, or purchase a data bundle.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number and country.
  • Phone-number verification records and verification status. Never send us your password, one-time verification code, MoMo PIN or card security code.
  • Wallet, payment, order and transaction details needed to operate the service, including references, amounts, statuses and the recipient number for a bundle.
  • Messages you send to customer support.
  • Basic technical information needed for website operation, security and troubleshooting, such as browser/device information and session storage.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify phone numbers, process wallet payments and data orders, show transaction history, provide support, prevent fraud and maintain the website.

3. Service providers

To provide the service, relevant information may be processed by our hosting and database provider (Supabase), payment provider (Xtopay), SMS verification provider, and data-bundle fulfilment supplier. They receive only information needed for their role and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

NetVanta may also use Meta technologies, such as the Meta Pixel, to measure website activity and advertising performance. Your browser or platform settings may provide controls over cookies and advertising activity.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not publish your account details. Information may be shared with service providers described above, or disclosed where required to comply with law, protect users, investigate fraud, or protect the service.

5. Storage and security

We use access controls and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to operate accounts, resolve disputes, meet recordkeeping obligations and protect the service.

6. Your choices and requests

You can keep your account details up to date through your account where those controls are available. To ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information, contact us at cmgnetvanta@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, accounting, payment disputes or legal obligations.

7. Children

NetVanta is not intended for children who are not legally able to enter into the relevant transactions. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services or obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page. Questions can be sent to cmgnetvanta@gmail.com.
