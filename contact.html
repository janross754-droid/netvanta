Contact NetVanta

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Need help with your NetVanta account, wallet, payment or data order? Contact our support team using the details below.

Customer support

Email: cmgnetvanta@gmail.com

When contacting us, include your account email and the relevant order or payment reference so we can locate the issue. Do not email your password, one-time verification code, MoMo PIN, card security code or full card number.

What we can help with

  • Account access and profile information.
  • Wallet deposits and payment verification.
  • Data order status, delivery questions and refund reviews.
  • Privacy requests and website feedback.

Before contacting us

Check your account's transaction history for wallet deposits and your order history for bundle status. An order marked pending or processing is still being handled and is not necessarily a failed order.

NetVanta support will never ask you to disclose your password, OTP, MoMo PIN or card security code.
