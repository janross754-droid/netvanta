Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how NetVanta handles wallet refunds for data-bundle orders and payment issues.

1. Pending and processing orders

If an order is marked pending or processing, it is still being checked or fulfilled. We do not automatically refund an order just because it is taking time. Please check your order history for status updates before placing the same order again.

2. Confirmed supplier refunds

When the fulfilment supplier confirms that an order has been refunded, NetVanta processes a credit of the order's charged retail amount back to the customer's NetVanta wallet. The refund process is designed to avoid crediting the same order more than once. The wallet transaction history is the place to confirm that the credit has posted.

3. Failed or disputed orders

A status labelled failed does not, by itself, always mean that the supplier has confirmed a refund. If an order appears failed but no wallet refund is shown, contact support with the order reference. We will review the supplier result and payment/order records. Do not send your password, OTP, MoMo PIN or card security code.

4. Completed orders

Once a bundle has been successfully delivered to the phone number entered at checkout, the order is generally not eligible for a refund because the service has been fulfilled. Please check the network and recipient number carefully before confirming.

5. Wallet top-ups and payment issues

If a payment provider reports a successful payment but your wallet is not credited, contact us with the payment reference, amount and approximate time. We will verify the payment status with the available transaction records. A pending or failed payment is not treated as a completed wallet deposit until verified.

6. How to request help

Email cmgnetvanta@gmail.com and include your account email, order or payment reference, and a short description. Never include passwords, OTPs, MoMo PINs or full card details.

7. Processing

We review requests as promptly as reasonably possible. Actual timing can depend on supplier and payment-provider confirmation. We do not promise a fixed processing time where a third party must first confirm the transaction.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy as payment and fulfilment processes change. The current version will remain available on this page.
